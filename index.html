How to use the research map

Select a site to see research

Click the number or site tag to view papers in that area. The card lists authors, institutions, models, and cells; When opened, you can view the main results, disease significance, and scope of conclusions.

For the same site, how do various studies explain it?

Click any anatomical site, embryonic region, or reference area to enter the corresponding study map. You can switch between location and question, click 'Object Highlight' related literature, click 'Connect to view evidence'. Reviews, methods, and preprints are displayed separately. Return to Overview Retain original location and filter.

Understanding local studies from an overall framework

“Research Framework" displays five interconnected explanatory frameworks. Click on a section to view core claims, review authors, relevant sites, and original research. The framework positioning of the site page allows you to filter the same question or return to the overall chart. Connecting lines is the reading organization of this page and does not represent a unified pathway or cell lineage. Six types of cell evidence questions are kept in the folded tool.

View research lines by author

“Team Index" summarizes papers from the same research line. Clicking on the paper will open the details; After closing, return to the original location and keep filtering conditions.

Check species, periods, and conditions

Display the model line by line for the same paper. When selecting species and stages, the same line must meet the criteria simultaneously. In vitro is the experimental condition; Third generation cultivation and postnatal P3 are indicated separately.

Distinguish between direct research and reference

Embryo studies are listed separately; maxilla, cranial suture, long bones, and tendons of the limbs are placed in reference areas. The positions and gene names shown in the figures are for literature navigation and cannot be used to read real cell coordinates or complete classification. Preprints are hidden by default and can be checked for display.

Compare and record

Select up to 4 articles to add to the "Discussion Comparison". Notes are saved in the current browser; Save the discussion version HTML to take the notes away. Currently filtered literature can be exported as a table.

The page can be used offline; opening the original text requires an internet connection. Save HTML containing maps, literature entries, and notes, but not the full thesis.

Shortcuts: / Search; Esc to close details, descriptions, or exit the presentation.
